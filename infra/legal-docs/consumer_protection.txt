CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT 2019 - COMPLETE GUIDE

WHO IS A CONSUMER:
Any person who buys goods or avails services for personal use (not for
commercial resale) is a consumer.

WHERE TO FILE COMPLAINT (based on value):
- District Consumer Commission: Up to Rs. 1 Crore
- State Consumer Commission: Rs. 1 Crore to Rs. 10 Crore
- National Consumer Commission: Above Rs. 10 Crore
- Online: edaakhil.nic.in (National Consumer Helpline: 1915)

WHO CAN FILE:
- The consumer themselves
- Any voluntary consumer association
- Central or State Government
- One or more consumers on behalf of many (class action)

TIME LIMIT:
Complaint must be filed within 2 years from the date of cause of action.
Courts may condone delay for sufficient cause.

FILING FEE:
- Up to Rs. 5 lakh: Rs. 200
- Rs. 5 lakh to Rs. 10 lakh: Rs. 400
- Rs. 10 lakh to Rs. 20 lakh: Rs. 500
- Rs. 20 lakh to Rs. 50 lakh: Rs. 2000
- Rs. 50 lakh to Rs. 1 crore: Rs. 4000

DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
1. Proof of purchase (bill, receipt, invoice)
2. Warranty card (if applicable)
3. Communication with seller (emails, chats)
4. Product defect evidence (photos/videos)
5. Medical records (if physical harm caused)

RELIEF AVAILABLE:
- Replacement of defective goods
- Full refund of price paid
- Compensation for physical/mental suffering
- Removal of defects from goods
- Withdrawal of hazardous goods from market
- Punitive damages

E-COMMERCE RULES 2020:
- Return/refund policy must be displayed
- Grievance officer must be designated
- 48-hour acknowledgement of complaints mandatory
- Resolve within 1 month
