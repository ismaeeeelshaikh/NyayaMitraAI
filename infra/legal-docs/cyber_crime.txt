CYBER CRIME IN INDIA - COMPLETE GUIDE

TYPES OF CYBER CRIMES AND SECTIONS:

1. ONLINE FINANCIAL FRAUD:
- Section 66C IT Act: Identity theft - 3 years + Rs. 1 lakh fine
- Section 66D IT Act: Cheating by personation - 3 years + Rs. 1 lakh fine
- Section 420 IPC: Cheating

2. HACKING/UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS:
- Section 43 IT Act: Damage to computer systems - compensation
- Section 66 IT Act: Computer related offences - 3 years + Rs. 5 lakh fine

3. ONLINE HARASSMENT/CYBERSTALKING:
- Section 66A IT Act: Sending offensive messages (use with 354D IPC)
- Section 354D IPC: Stalking including online stalking - 3 years
- Section 507 IPC: Criminal intimidation by anonymous communication

4. OBSCENE CONTENT/MORPHING:
- Section 66E IT Act: Violation of privacy - 3 years + Rs. 2 lakh fine
- Section 67 IT Act: Publishing obscene material - 5 years + Rs. 10 lakh fine
- Section 67A IT Act: Sexually explicit material - 7 years + Rs. 10 lakh fine

5. SEXTORTION (Most Common):
- Section 67A + 66E IT Act
- Section 384 IPC: Extortion
- DO NOT PAY THE MONEY - report immediately
- Block the person on all platforms
- Save screenshots as evidence

HOW TO REPORT CYBER CRIME:

METHOD 1 - NATIONAL PORTAL (Fastest):
- Website: cybercrime.gov.in
- 24/7 available
- Report anonymously also possible
- Helpline: 1930 (call immediately for financial fraud)

METHOD 2 - LOCAL POLICE:
- Cyber Crime Cell at nearest police station
- Bring: Device, screenshots, transaction details

METHOD 3 - COURT:
- Direct complaint to Magistrate if police doesn't act
- Section 156(3) CrPC: Court directs police to register FIR

CRITICAL STEPS - DO IMMEDIATELY:
1. Do NOT delete anything - screenshots, chats, emails are evidence
2. Call 1930 IMMEDIATELY for online financial fraud (golden hour)
3. Do NOT pay money to extortionists
4. Block the fraudster but AFTER taking screenshots
5. Change passwords immediately

EVIDENCE TO PRESERVE:
- Screenshots of conversation
- URL of fraudulent website
- Email headers
- Transaction IDs/UTR numbers
- Device on which fraud occurred
