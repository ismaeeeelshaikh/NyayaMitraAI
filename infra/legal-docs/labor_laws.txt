INDIAN LABOR LAWS - COMPLETE WORKERS GUIDE

KEY RIGHTS OF EVERY EMPLOYEE:

1. SALARY/WAGES:
- Minimum Wages Act 1948: State sets minimum wage by industry
- Payment of Wages Act 1936: Wages must be paid by 7th (small firms) or 10th of month
- Equal Remuneration Act: Equal pay for equal work (no gender discrimination)
- Unlawful Deductions: Employer cannot deduct more than 50% of wages

2. PROVIDENT FUND (PF):
- Employees Provident Funds Act 1952
- Mandatory for establishments with 20+ employees
- Employee contributes 12% of basic salary; Employer matches
- PF can be withdrawn after retirement, or partially for emergency
- Contact: EPFO helpline 1800-118-005

3. GRATUITY:
- Payment of Gratuity Act 1972
- Applicable after 5 years of continuous service
- Formula: (Last salary x 15 x years of service) / 26
- Must be paid within 30 days of leaving
- Maximum: Rs. 20 Lakhs

4. ESI (Employee State Insurance):
- For employees earning up to Rs. 21,000/month
- Free medical treatment at ESI hospitals
- Sickness benefit: 70% of wages for 91 days

5. MATERNITY BENEFIT:
- Maternity Benefit Act 1961
- 26 weeks paid maternity leave (for first 2 children)
- Cannot be terminated during pregnancy

6. TERMINATION RULES:
- Industrial Disputes Act 1947
- 100+ employees: Government permission needed to terminate
- All employees: 1 month notice or pay in lieu of notice
- Retrenchment compensation: 15 days salary per year of service
- No termination during: maternity, strike notice period, inquiry

HOW TO FILE LABOR COMPLAINT:
1. First give written complaint to employer (keep copy)
2. If unresolved: File with Labour Commissioner's office
3. Labour Court: For unfair termination, wage disputes
4. Industrial Tribunal: For large-scale disputes

DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
- Appointment letter
- Last 6 months salary slips
- Bank statements showing salary credits
- PF passbook
- Any written communication with employer

HELPLINES:
- Labour Commissioner: Contact state labor department
- EPFO: 1800-118-005
- National Labor Helpline: 14441
